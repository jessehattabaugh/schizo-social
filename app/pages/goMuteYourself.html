Go Mute Yourself

There are a lot of people on the Fediverse; it's a very open and diverse place, so there are going to be people who disagree with you on a lot of things. Have you ever wished that you could just know which people are Nazis so you could mute them all? Or better still; let them know that they can block or mute you! It's not like they want to talk to you anyway.

This is an app that lets you do that. You answer questions about yourself, and indicate which answers you would like to auto-mute, or block. Then you can download a list of users to mute and block and you can import those files into mastodon.

How it works

First, you need to sign in with your Mastodon account. This does not make any changes to your Mastodon account,or posts on your behalf. It is simply to authenticate the answers you provide.

Answering questions

Then you answer questions about yourself. These questions are designed to help you identify people who you would like to mute or block. For example "Did Hitler have some good ideas". If you answer yes to this question, you might want to mute or block people who answer no. If you answer no, you might want to mute or block people who answer yes.

If you have an idea for a question, you can submit it as a suggestion. Questions should be simple yes/no questions under 140 characters.

You can easily skip a question if you don't want to answer it, or if you don't understand it.

Downloading lists

Once you have answered all the questions you are interested in answering, you can download the following lists.

  • mute
  • block
  • follow
  • some other named list

You can adjust settings like thresholds to exclude/include people who only disagree with one or two things to narrow or broaden the lists.

If the list is too big to be imported it will display a warning and you will need to adjust the thresholds until it is small enough to make the warning go away.
